## SUS pack and the data model (batch 2)

Date: 2026-11-03.

11. *Where the later additions of the prompt were built.* The addenda 2–6 change the data model and the engine (equipment types, monitoring level, backup hours, pump fields, urban zones, mobile network, heat, cold, cold chain, realtime fields, hypothesis, handover fields). Both packs' patients are generated by the same generator, so the model and the engine were built in batch 2 together with the SUS pack, with their tests; the pages that show them follow in batches 3–5 (patient list, equipment, capacity, dependencies, overview). Rejected: building the SUS pack on the old model and regenerating its patients later (it would have meant two generations of data files and tests).
12. *The Karolinska patients were regenerated* (same seed 20261102) under the new model: the old equipment types no longer exist, so ids and positions are as before only by construction of the streams, not by guarantee. Rejected: keeping the old file with a mapping layer.
13. *Equipment weights.* Added to the source's weights: Droppställning 8 % (prompt), the oxygen weight 10 % split 8 % stationary and 2 % portable (prompt), Hemrespirator 3 % and Hemdialys 5 % removed (addendum 5). The prompt does not say where the freed 3 % and the new 8 % go; "none" takes the remainder: 50 %. A patient has at most one type (the source drew a second, independent type for dialysis).
14. *Battery hours.* Väska 12–24 h (phone or hub), stationary concentrator 0 h, portable 2–5 h, as in addendum 3. Pump has no battery (addendum 5 overrides addendum 3's "Pump: 4–8 h"). The battery time counted in the priority is battery plus reserve (`batteryHours + backupHours`).
15. *Unit selector.* The prompt says the menu lists "SUS, Lund, Malmö and, when the Karolinska pack is selected, Karolinska, Solna, Huddinge" and that choosing a site of a pack switches pack. Read literally: SUS, Lund and Malmö are always listed (choosing one while Karolinska is active switches to SUS); Karolinska's units are listed only while that pack is active, and the way back to Karolinska is the pack switch in the Sjukhus hemma mode bar (works both ways). Rejected: always listing both packs.
16. *Site ids.* The SUS sites are `suslund` and `susmalmo` (not `lund`/`malmo`) so that they never collide with the zone ids `lund` and `malmo`, whose graph vertices are `zone:<id>`.
17. *Fixture topology for SUS.* The hand-made road fixture connects the two halves with an E22 way from Lund Södra to Arlöv; the ways at Lund Södra are all within 300 m of the node, so closing them cuts Staffanstorp off from Lund. The real data will replace the fixture when `fetch-osm.yml` has run. An attempt to fetch the roads from Overpass during the build was stopped on purpose: the prompt asks for fixtures until the workflow has run.
18. *Presets.* Five demo scenarios per pack instead of three: the source's bridge, power and water, plus Värmebölja and Kyla med elavbrott (addendum 5: "Båda får presets"). SUS power hits Rosengård (urban, network falls after 1 h) and Dalby (not urban, 4 h) so that both rules show. The cold preset includes a power outage in its zone, because the cold only counts together with one.
19. *Heat actions.* Addendum 5 says "Kylpåse" and "Kortare infusion" are logged and "ger 12 h extra". Read as applying to each of them. A pump patient in a heat zone becomes a row only when the 6 h or 12 h threshold has passed; patients of 80 and older are affected from the start.
20. *Network down is registered-only in the impact, signal-wide in the realtime.* An outage started from the Demo popover ("Elavbrott utan registrering") changes the simulated signals (contact, batteries, "utan kontakt de senaste 30 min", the hypothesis) but not the impact, which counts only registered disruptions. Registering a suspected outage dates the record from the signal (first silent contact minus the network delay). The hypothesis needs at least 60 % of the zone's patients silent for at least 10 minutes with last contacts within the same 15 minutes, and no registered disruption of any kind in the zone.
21. *Cold chain.* One stock per pack (`unitStock`), counted down from the earliest of a supplier disruption and the moment every road within 250 m of any site of the pack is closed. Rejected: one stock per site (the prompt gives the pack one `unitStock`).
22. *Bulk contact* skips patients whose zone is out of contact (a phone call cannot reach them); the panel proposes Besök inom 2 h or Ta in for them (addendum 4).
23. *DATA § 5 sources.* The prompt names the sources for the equipment and the mobile network but gives no dates or links; they are listed as S-E1…S-E6 with what they were cited for, `reported`, and the section says that this build did not re-read them. The 6 h and 12 h heat thresholds are marked `estimate`.
24. *Malmö grid owner.* The notice for the power outage in Rosengård names "Elnätsägaren" because DATA.md cannot establish whether E.ON or Kraftringen owns the grid there (the prompt's own fallback).

## Overview, handovers, equipment, capacity and dependencies (batch 4)

Date: 2026-11-03.

32. *Handovers are a record.* "Ta in" appends a record `{ id, patientId, at, site, reasons, priority }` to `home.handovers`; the status chain of a patient resets when the disruption ends, but the list "Överlämningar till sjukhus" does not (a decision taken is history). A second "Ta in" for the same patient replaces the first. The export file is `medoma-center/handover/1` (SPEC § 7), named `overlamningar-<pack>-<hhmm>.json`; the button is disabled while the list is empty. `exportedAt` is the demo clock on the demo day.
33. *Realtime cards read the whole signal*, registered or not: "Utan kontakt nu" counts patients whose zone has no mobile network now; "Kortaste batteri (hub)" shows the lowest hub battery and the first patient with it (all patients share the same charge times, so the value is the same for everyone until an outage stops charging); "Nästa pumpbyte" counts pump patients whose change falls within 2 h. The counts link to Sjukhus hemma (equipment for the pump card).
34. *Equipment.* Total = in use + the pack's stock; the stock "Syrgaskoncentrator +2" is split one stationary and one portable. Each row carries the stock's confidence chip (illustrative). In preparedness mode "affected" counts patients of the type that are affected (any ground), and Batteri is the earliest `batteryEmptyAt − clock` among them (battery plus reserve), "–" when none has a deadline.
35. *Capacity.* The whole page is synthetic and deterministic (DATA § 5.9). The seven-day strip is clickable and selects the day the cards and rows show. "Planerad inskrivning" is drawn (2–6 a day), "Planerad utskrivning" counts patients whose planned discharge date is that day. In preparedness mode a future day covered by a disruption with an end time shows affected visits scaled from today's share (an estimate); the extra driving time is shown for today only because only today's visits have detours. Extra km come from the same `extraKm` the patient list will show with "+N km" in batch 5.
36. *Dependencies.* Nineteen rows (the prompt's eighteen plus Medoma Center). The status is computed per row in `lib/dependencies.ts` from the same disruption state as the impact; where the prompt gives no time (refrigerated medicines 4 h, door phone, lift, heating 6 h) the text says "antagande" and the number of patients is "går inte att räkna" where nobody can say who is concerned. The page loads in preparedness mode.
37. *Silent patients line.* "N utan kontakt de senaste 30 min" is shown in the Sjukhus hemma panel also when no disruption is registered (it is the first signal), computed from the simulated last contact of every patient.

## Closing a road and the detour (batch 5)

Date: 2026-11-03.

38. *One record per tap.* A tap in "Stäng vägar" makes one disruption record with `targetKind: 'ways'` whose target is the OSM way ids joined by commas (the tapped way and every way sharing its name or ref within 250 m of the tapped point, both carriageways). A second tap on any way of the record opens all of them again (one "Öppnade" entry). Rejected: one record per way (a road of six ways would take six "end now" clicks). "Block whole road" still closes every way with the name, by road key, as before; closing a single way by id remains an action of the store (`toggleBlockedWay`) but no longer has a map gesture.
39. *"Within 250 m"* is measured from the tapped point to the road graph's edges (straight segments between graph vertices), not to the full way geometry; a way that is long between two vertices is found when any part of an edge passes within 250 m. The rule matches by name or by ref (a differing ref with the same name is a separate road only for "Block whole road", as in the source).
40. *Label.* "<name> vid <place>", where the name is the way's name, else its ref, else "OSM <id>", and the place is the zone whose centre is nearest the tap (the pack has no list of localities, only zones).
41. *Detour.* `extraKm` is the whole-kilometre difference between the shortest path now and the baseline from the sites in scope; the row shows "+N km" after "Nås via omväg" and the summary line "Omväg: median N km, högst M km". The median of an even number is the rounded mean of the two middle values.

## Map, polish and screenshots (batch 6)

Date: 2026-11-03.

42. *Start view.* The map fits the box around every zone circle and the sites of the selected pack (24 px padding); infrastructure nodes, markers and arrow tips outside that are left to the user. Switching pack fits again.
43. *Power lines.* Ways of `power.json` with at least 220 kV are merged into one line when they share an end point and a voltage tier; the one label ("400 kV", "220 kV", "400/220 kV") sits on the vertex nearest the centroid of the line (a vertex of the line, so it is always on it) and shows from zoom level 11. 70 kV and lower are not drawn. 400 kV uses the new token `--color-power-400` (yellow; one value per theme); 220 kV is 1.5 px in `--color-text-muted`. Rejected: labelling every OSM way (a line is dozens of ways).
44. *Water links* are 1 px, dotted (`1 4`), 50 % opacity and have no tooltip or label. *Patient dots* are 3 px; neutral at 30 % opacity in light mode, `--color-text-secondary` (light grey) at 70 % in dark mode.
45. *Indicator contrast.* White on `--color-red` (`#e33026`) is 4.45:1, below the 4.5:1 floor, so the indicator uses the new token `--color-indicator-bg` (`#c42a21`, 5.7:1) in both themes; a unit test computes the ratio from the stylesheet.
