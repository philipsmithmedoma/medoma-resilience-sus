# DATA.md – Medoma Center data

Data lives in data packs (`src/data/packs/`), reached through the organisation (`organisations.ts`), never in components. Every figure in a pack carries confidence, source (a key in the pack's source list) and date; the UI renders the confidence wherever the figure is shown. Patients, teams, visits, staff and notices are synthetic.

## 1. Confidence classes

| Class | Meaning | Chip |
|---|---|---|
| `verified` | A figure or fact from a public source that was read and can be cited; the source key is shown | none (a link "Källa S-x" on request) |
| `reported` | Reported to Medoma by the customer or read from a map service (Google Places, OpenStreetMap), not verified against an open source | "Uppgift" / "Reported" |
| `estimate` | Derived or assumed from a stated basis; the basis text is shown | "Estimat" / "Estimate" |
| `illustrative` | A scenario value written for the prototype (patient counts, battery times, stock, notices); never real data | "Illustrativt" / "Illustrative" |

A sum takes the weakest class of its parts. The overview cards are all `illustrative`. A node whose position has no matching OpenStreetMap object keeps a fallback `estimate` (or, while only the fixture is present, `illustrative`) and says so.

## 2. OpenStreetMap

Source: OpenStreetMap via the Overpass API, queries in `scripts/overpass/<pack>/*.overpassql`, run on the date recorded in `src/data/packs/geo/<pack>/README.md`. Licence ODbL; "© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL" is shown on the map and in the README. Classification: road geometry, bridge and tunnel tags, substation positions and line voltages are `reported` (OSM, date). Patient street placement is `illustrative`. Completeness is not verified against NVDB or Svenska kraftnät; where a named crossing is missing from a test's crossing set, the test reveals it and the set is extended, which is logged.

Files per pack (`scripts/fetch-osm.mjs <pack>`, Douglas–Peucker 8 m, only the properties listed):

* `roads.json` – ways with `highway` in motorway, trunk, primary, secondary and their links inside the pack's bbox. Properties `id`, `highway`, `name`, `ref`, `bridge`, `tunnel`, `oneway`, `lanes`.
* `streets.json` – residential and living streets within the zone radius of each zone centre (one query, one `around` clause per zone). Properties `id`, `name`. Used only to place patients.
* `power.json` – `power=substation` (centroids) and `power=line` with voltage 400000 or 220000 inside the bbox. Properties `id`, `power`, `name`, `operator`, `voltage`.
* `water.json` – `man_made=water_works` (centroids). Properties `id`, `name`, `operator`.

## 5. Equipment, monitoring, mobile network, messages and time

All figures of this section were supplied with the commissioning prompt together with the names of their sources; this build did not re-read the source documents, so they are `reported` and the source is named in the key list of § 5.8. Battery and reserve times per patient are `illustrative` within the intervals given.

### 5.1 Equipment types

| Type | Electricity | Battery (illustrative per patient) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Droppställning / IV stand | no | – | – |
| Pump (elastomeric) | no; 15–30 W devices with 4–8 h recommended reserve apply to electric pumps, which the prototype does not model | – | S-E3 |
| Syrgaskoncentrator, stationär | yes, 310–350 W, no battery: stops at once in a power outage | 0 h; reserve tubes 4–10 h | S-E1 |
| Syrgaskoncentrator, bärbar | yes, 40–90 W, own batteries | 2–5 h; reserve tubes 4–10 h | S-E1 |
| Väska för egenmonitorering / Self-monitoring kit | the sensor lasts days; the phone or hub 12–24 h; the data goes dark when the mobile network falls | 12–24 h | S-E2 |

The platform's equipment list has the types Droppställning, Pump, Syrgaskoncentrator and Väska för egenmonitorering. The source repository's types map as IV infusion → Pump, Monitoring → Väska för egenmonitorering, Oxygen → Syrgaskoncentrator (split here into stationary and portable). Hemrespirator (home ventilator) and Hemdialys (home dialysis) are not in the platform's list and are removed from this prototype and from all rules.

### 5.2 Patient generation rules

Weights of the main type: none 50 %, Väska 25 %, Droppställning 8 %, stationary concentrator 8 %, Pump 7 %, portable concentrator 2 %. Position: a random vertex of a random street within the zone radius (the nearest zone centre wins). Age 24–91, visit today 40 %, patient app logged in 60 %, enrolment 1–14 days before the demo day and planned discharge 3–21 days after, all `illustrative`. Monitoring: Väska patients are `spot` in 60 % and `continuous` in 40 %; continuous splits arrhythmia 35 %, sepsis 40 %, respiratory 20 %, other 5 %; level and reason are `illustrative`, and the platform's own representation of monitoring level is **to verify**. Personal numbers are synthetic `YYYYMMDD-XXXX`: the birth date matches the age and the last four digits are random with the check digit forced to fail, so they can never be real. Seed: SUS 20261103 (the removed Karolinska pack used 20261102).

### 5.3 Pumps, antibiotics and heat

Pump patients carry an antibiotic (illustrative distribution): piperacillin/tazobaktam 25 %, cefazolin 20 %, flukloxacillin 20 %, bensylpenicillin 10 %, meropenem 10 %, cefepim 10 %, ceftazidim 5 %. `heatSensitive` is set for meropenem, cefepim and ceftazidim, which the stability overviews for elastomeric pumps (S-E4) describe as the limiting ones at room or higher temperature. The pump is changed every 24 h at a per-patient time of day. Heatwave rules: heat-sensitive pump patients critical after 6 h, other pump patients after 12 h; "Kylpåse" and "Kortare infusion" each give 12 h extra. The 6 h and 12 h thresholds are assumptions (`estimate`), not published limits.

### 5.4 Mobile network and urban zones

In a power outage the mobile network is assumed to fall after 1 h in an urban zone and after 4 h elsewhere, following PTS's requirements on backup power in the mobile networks (S-E5; 1 h urban, 4 h rural as stated in the prompt). Urban zones: Lund, Malmö centrum, Limhamn, Hyllie, Rosengård and Arlöv; all other zones are not urban (the urban zones of the removed Karolinska pack are in `docs/archive/karolinska-infra.md`). The team's own app is assumed to fall with the network.

### 5.5 Messages and time

The notices in `homeMessages` are illustrative texts written for the prototype. The organisations named (Trafikverket, the grid owner and VA SYD) are real; the senders are fictitious and the events never happened. Visit slots and team bases are illustrative. The clock is the demo clock, minutes since 00:00 of the demo day (14:40 at start); disruptions carry start and expected end in clock minutes.

### 5.6 Realtime fields and the device event

`lastContactAt`, `hubBatteryPct`, `phoneBatteryPct`, `lastMeasurementAt` and `pumpNextChangeAt` are simulated against the clock (SPEC § 6): contact every 5 minutes, hub battery −4 % and phone −6 % an hour, both charged to 100 % at 07:00 and 22:00, charging stopped in a power outage, contact stopped when the network has fallen. They are `illustrative`. The interface they stand for is the device event of SPEC § 7. **No supplier delivers these events to the platform today.** Corsano (CardioWatch 287; the manufacturer's knowledge base gives 1–5 days of battery, and up to 15 days with sparser settings, S-E2) and WARD 24/7 (no published battery times; **to verify**, S-E6) are the first to ask.

### 5.7 Medoma Center and the hospital's power

Medoma Center runs in the hospital's control centre on the hospital's backup power and is assumed never to fail in a power outage. What fails in the home is Medoma Care and the monitoring, in the field Medoma Go.

### 5.9 Capacity (synthetic)

Shifts per team and day: four nurses day, two evening, one night, one doctor day, 8 h each (prompt). Today's home visits are the pack's visits (before 15:00 a day-shift visit, later an evening visit); later days a share of the enrolled patients (about 40 %, three quarters on the day shift). Video calls, phone calls, clinic visits and administrative tasks are drawn deterministically per team, day, profession and shift within these ranges (illustrative, written for the prototype, not data from any service): nurse day clinic 1–3, video 2–5, administrative 3–6, phone 4–8; nurse evening 0–1, 1–2, 1–2, 2–4; nurse night video 0–1, administrative 0–1, phone 0–2; doctor day 2–4, 3–6, 2–4, 2–4. Planned admission 2–6 a day, assessment is the number of patients under assessment. The extra driving time of a shift is the extra kilometres of the detours of its affected visits divided by 40 km/h.
